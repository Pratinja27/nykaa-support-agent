Topic: Festive Sale Cancellation and Returns Policy

Orders placed during the Festive Sale event can only be canceled within 2 hours of placement, provided the status is still 'Placed'. Once an order transitions to 'Processing' or 'Shipped', cancellations are strictly not permitted. 

Festive beauty bundles, limited-edition gift sets, and flash sale items are marked non-returnable unless received in a damaged or defective state. Any damage or discrepancy must be reported within 24 hours of delivery with unboxing video proof. 

Standard delivery timelines for Festive Sale orders are extended to 5 to 7 business days due to high order volume.
